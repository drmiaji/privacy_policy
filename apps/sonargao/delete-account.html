How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Al Quran & Dua account and associated app data, please email us at: bmiaji@gmail.com

Please include the following information in your email:

  • The email address used to sign in to the app.
  • A short message saying: “Please delete my Al Quran & Dua account and associated data.”

Data that will be deleted

When your request is verified, we will delete account-linked app data stored in Firebase, such as:

  • Your app account record, where applicable.
  • Synced bookmarks and notes.
  • Synced notification/message records.
  • Firebase messaging token records linked to your account or device, where applicable.

Data stored only on your device

Some data is stored locally on your device, such as settings, reading history, search history, downloaded Quran audio/resources, and daily activity progress. You can remove this data by clearing app data in Android settings or uninstalling the app.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained where required for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, or records managed by another provider. For example, Google Play purchase records are managed by Google Play and may be retained according to Google’s policies.

Processing time

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days.
