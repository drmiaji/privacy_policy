Privacy Policy for Al Quran & Dua

Effective date: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Al Quran & Dua ("the app") handles information when you use the app. The app is published by Dr. Abdul-Baten Miaji.

Information We Collect

The app may collect or process the following information, depending on which features you use:

  • Account information: If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication may process your name, email address, profile information, and account identifier.
  • App content and activity: Bookmarks, notes, read history, search history, notification history, selected settings, daily activity progress, and similar app data may be stored locally on your device. If sync is enabled or account features are used, some of this data may be stored in Firebase under your app account.
  • Location: The app may request approximate or precise location to calculate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location is used for these features and may be saved locally as prayer/Qibla settings.
  • Notifications and device information: Firebase Cloud Messaging may use a device messaging token to deliver notifications. The app may store notification records and basic device information needed for messaging or sync.
  • Diagnostics and analytics: Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics may collect crash logs, performance information, app interactions, device type, operating system version, and similar diagnostic data.
  • Purchases: If you use support or premium features through Google Play Billing, Google Play processes the payment. The app may receive purchase status, product identifiers, and purchase tokens to verify access.
  • Downloaded content: Quran audio, translations, tafsir, and other resources may be downloaded and stored on your device for offline use.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide Quran reading, dua, zikr, prayer time, Qibla, reminder, and offline reading features.
  • Save your preferences, bookmarks, notes, reading progress, and daily activity progress.
  • Sync account-linked data when you choose to use sign-in or sync features.
  • Send notifications that you enable, including reminders, prayer alerts, and app messages.
  • Process or verify Google Play purchases.
  • Improve reliability, fix crashes, and understand general app usage.

Third-Party Services

The app uses third-party services that may process data according to their own policies:

  • Google Firebase Authentication
  • Google Firebase Cloud Firestore
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Google Firebase Analytics
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Sign-In

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be processed by Google/Firebase and Google Play as service providers for authentication, sync, notifications, analytics, crash reporting, and billing. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the app.

Permissions

The app may request permissions for:

  • Internet and network state: To download content, connect to Firebase, and use online features.
  • Notifications: To show reminders, prayer alerts, and app messages.
  • Location: To calculate prayer times and Qibla direction when you use those features.
  • Exact alarms, vibration, boot completed, wake lock, and foreground services: To support reminders, prayer alerts, and background audio playback.
  • Storage/media access: To save or play downloaded audio and other app resources on Android versions where this permission is required.
  • Google Play Billing: To support purchases or donations made through Google Play.

Local Storage

Many app features work by storing data locally on your device, including settings, bookmarks, reading history, search history, downloaded resources, daily activity progress, and notification history. You can clear some local data from inside the app or by clearing app data in Android settings.

Data Retention and Deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, uninstall the app, or clear app data. Account-linked Firebase data remains until it is deleted through the app's account/data deletion option or until you request deletion by contacting us. Some records, such as Google Play purchase records, may be retained by Google according to Google Play policies.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards for the app and rely on Google Firebase and Google Play security controls for hosted services. Data sent to Firebase or Google services is transmitted using encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's Privacy

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can review and delete it where appropriate.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
